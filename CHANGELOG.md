# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Official public multiplayer release with two to six drivers, private room codes, independent vehicle selection, reconnect and host migration. LAN rooms remain available.
- Drive Alpine Proving Ground or Pineglass Run 2.0 in the Ferrari F40 or Nissan GT-R R35 Rally Edition. Multiplayer offers Free Drive and the course-specific Best Lap or Best Stage Challenge.
- Includes checkpoint, sector and stage timing, revised track-limit validation, Pineglass recovery, synchronized remote cars and dust, and multiplayer grid and rendering improvements.

## 0.1.0

- Published the source, dual-car simulator, asset setup and contribution workflow.
- Current live gameplay includes the F40 and GT-R, interactive cockpits, showroom, keyboard controls, AWD, timing and track wear.
